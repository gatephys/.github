# GatePhys

**The Gateway Institute for Theoretical Physics**

GatePhys is a deep-tech research initiative focused on transforming advanced physical principles into patentable technological concepts.

We work at the intersection of theoretical physics, scientific AI, numerical simulation and deep-tech innovation. Our research process moves from first-principles reasoning to mathematical modelling, simulation-driven exploration and the development of technically defensible solution pathways.

## Research focus

* Theoretical and mathematical physics
* Quantum technologies and qubit concepts
* Scientific AI and numerical simulation
* Advanced materials and thermal systems
* Patentable deep-tech innovation
* Technology transfer and intellectual property strategy

## Mission

To identify novel physical mechanisms, develop patentable concepts and support the transition from theoretical insight to protectable technology.
